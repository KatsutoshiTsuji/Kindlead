Kindlead - Accessible Kindle Reader (NVDA Add-on)

Authors: Katsutoshi Tsuji (Debugon) & Antigravity

Overview

Kindlead is an NVDA add-on that automatically turns pages and captures books open in Kindle for PC (especially fixed-layout or image-only books that are inaccessible to screen readers) and converts them into fully accessible, structured HTML documents with heading hierarchy and clickable table of contents using Google Gemini Multimodal AI.

How to Use

  1. Get a free Gemini API key from Google AI Studio.
  2. Open the NVDA Menu (NVDA + N) → Preferences → Settings → Kindlead category, and paste your API key.
  3. Open Kindle for PC and display the beginning (cover or page 1) of the book you want to read.
  4. Open the NVDA Menu → Tools → Kindlead - Structured capture from Kindle book...
  5. Confirm the checklist and click "Start Capture". Keep your hands off the keyboard and mouse while Kindle turns pages automatically.
  6. Once capture and AI processing finish, a chime will play, and the structured HTML document will automatically open in your default browser for accessible reading.
